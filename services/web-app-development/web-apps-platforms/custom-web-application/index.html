What you get

  • A discovery document that maps your workflow, users and must-have features
  • Clickable screen designs you approve before development starts
  • A secure web application with logins and roles for staff, partners or customers
  • Integrations with tools you already use, such as WhatsApp, Tally, payment gateways or Google Sheets
  • Hosting setup on your own cloud account, with backups
  • Full source code and admin access handed over to you
  • Training, documentation and a support period after launch

  1. 1

Questions people ask

How long does it take to build?

A focused first version usually takes six to twelve weeks, depending on features and integrations. We break bigger projects into phases so you start using something useful early.

Do I own the code?

Yes. The source code, hosting account, domain and data are yours. We hand everything over and can keep supporting you, but you are never locked in.

I am not technical. Can I still manage this project?

Yes. You only need to explain how your business works and give feedback on screens. We explain decisions in plain language and never expect you to read code.

What happens after launch?

We offer a support and maintenance plan for fixes, updates and new features, or we can hand over to your own team with proper documentation.
