Questions people ask

How long does it take?

A standard setup for one location usually takes two to four weeks. Multiple branches, custom rules or integrations with existing software can take longer, and we will tell you upfront.

Can it work with my existing clinic or salon software?

Often yes. If your current software has an API or a way to import and export data, we connect to it. If not, we suggest the simplest workable option.

Who owns the customer data?

You do. The bookings, customer details and payment account are in your name, and you can export everything at any time.

Will this reduce no-shows?

Reminders and advance payments usually help a lot, but no system removes no-shows completely. We help you choose reminder timing and deposit rules that suit your customers.
