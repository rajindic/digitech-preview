Questions people ask

Which one should I choose: Zoho, Odoo or ERPNext?

It depends on your size, budget for licences, team comfort and how much customisation you need. We compare them for your case in a short discovery call and recommend honestly, even if you already have one.

How long does a typical setup take?

A focused setup of two or three modules usually takes three to six weeks. A larger rollout across sales, stock and accounts is planned in phases.

Who owns the account and data?

The software subscription or server is in your name, and all your data stays with you. We work with access you give us and hand over admin rights fully.

Can it work with Tally?

Yes, in most cases. We can sync invoices, ledgers or stock between the two, or help you decide whether Tally remains your main accounting tool.
