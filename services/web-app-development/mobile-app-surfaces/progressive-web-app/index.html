Progressive web app (installable web app)

An installable app your customers can add from your website, no app store needed

A full mobile app is a big step, and many customers will not download one. A progressive web app works from your website but can be installed on a phone's home screen, works on slow connections and can send notifications. Customers get an app-like experience in one tap, and you avoid app store delays.

Questions people ask

How is this different from a normal app?

It runs in the browser and is installed from your website, so there is no app store download or approval. It suits most booking, ordering and content needs.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, it can be installed on iPhone too, though some features such as notifications work slightly differently there.

How long does it take?

Usually four to ten weeks, depending on features.

Who owns it?

You do. The code, domain and hosting are all in your name.
