Questions people ask

Will I lose my Google rankings?

A careful migration with proper redirects keeps the risk low, but a short dip after any move is possible. We plan and track this closely.

Will customers need new passwords?

Usually yes, because passwords cannot be copied for security reasons. We help you send a friendly invite to reactivate accounts.

How long does it take?

Usually two to six weeks, depending on store size and custom features.

Will my store go offline?

No. Your old store keeps running until the new one is tested and ready.
