Questions people ask

Supabase or Firebase, which should I choose?

It depends on your app. Supabase suits structured data like orders, bookings and reports, while Firebase suits real-time features and quick mobile builds. We recommend one after the first call and explain why.

Who owns the accounts and code?

You do. The Supabase or Firebase project is created in your company account and all code lives in your repository.

How long will it take?

Most backends take two to five weeks, depending on how many features and user types your app has.

What happens after launch?

We can monitor and maintain it on a monthly plan, or hand it over fully to your own team with documentation.
