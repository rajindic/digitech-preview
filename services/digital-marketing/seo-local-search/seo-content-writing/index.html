Questions people ask

Who writes the content?

Experienced writers who research your topic and follow your brand voice. Every piece is edited before you see it.

What do I need to provide?

A short call about your business, and quick reviews to make sure facts and advice are correct, especially for medical or technical topics.

Who owns the content?

You do, fully, once it is delivered.

Will removing old posts hurt my site?

Done carefully, it usually helps. We redirect or merge pages so you keep any value they had.
