Questions people ask

How long does it take?

Most catalogues of up to a few dozen products are done in one to two weeks. Larger catalogues are done in batches, best sellers first.

What do I need to provide?

Seller account access or a catalogue export, plus product details and any claims you are allowed to make.

Will this put me on the first page?

Rankings depend on many things, including sales history, price, reviews and ads. Good listings improve your chances and help more visitors buy, and we show you the before-and-after numbers.

Can you also improve the images?

Yes. Images and A+ content are a separate service we often do together with listing copy.
