Shopify store optimisation, speed fixes

A faster, smoother Shopify store that turns more visitors into orders

A slow or clunky Shopify store loses shoppers before they even see your products, especially on mobile data. Too many apps, heavy images and theme bloat are the usual culprits. We clean up your theme, fix speed and Core Web Vitals issues and smooth out the path from product page to checkout, so shopping feels quick and easy.

What you get

  • A speed and Core Web Vitals report for your home, collection, product and cart pages
  • App clean-up, removing unused apps and leftover code
  • Image, font and script optimisation for faster loading on mobile
  • Theme tweaks to product pages, cart and navigation for easier buying
  • Trust elements such as COD and UPI badges, delivery estimates and reviews placed where they help
  • A before and after speed comparison
  • A list of further improvements, ranked by impact

How it works

  1. 1

    Audit the store

    We test your store on real mobile connections, review your apps and theme code and note where shoppers drop off.

  2. 2

    Fix the speed

    We remove dead code, compress images, lazy-load what can wait and replace heavy apps with lighter options where possible.

  3. 3

    Improve the journey

    We tidy up product pages, collections and the cart so shoppers find what they want and check out with less friction.

  4. 4

    Measure and hand over

    We share the before and after results and a list of next steps, and keep a backup of your original theme.

Questions people ask

How long does it take?

Usually one to three weeks, depending on the size of your store and how many apps are involved.

Will my store go down while you work?

No. We work on a copy of your theme and publish changes only after testing, so customers keep shopping as usual.

What do I need to provide?

Staff access to your Shopify store and a list of apps you consider essential. We check with you before removing anything.

Will my speed score hit a perfect mark?

Not always, as some apps and third-party scripts are outside our control. We aim for a clear, real improvement in loading time on mobile and explain any trade-offs.
