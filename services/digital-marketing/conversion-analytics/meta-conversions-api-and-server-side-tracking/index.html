Meta Conversions API and server-side tracking

Send Meta and Google accurate conversion data even when browsers block pixels

Ad blockers, iPhone privacy settings and slow pages mean the Meta Pixel misses many of your real leads and sales. When Meta cannot see your results, it shows your ads to the wrong people. We set up the Conversions API and server-side tracking, so your website sends conversions directly and reliably, without double counting, and your ads have better data to learn from.

What you get

  • A check of your current pixel setup and how many events are being missed
  • Meta Conversions API set up for leads, sign-ups and purchases
  • Server-side Tag Manager container hosted in your own cloud account, where it suits your setup
  • Deduplication so each conversion is counted once, not twice
  • Customer details hashed and sent securely to improve event match quality
  • Offline conversions from your CRM, such as leads that became customers, where possible
  • A test report and a guide to checking event quality in Events Manager

How it works

  1. 1

    Audit the pixel

    We review the events Meta and Google receive today and compare them with your real leads and orders.

  2. 2

    Plan the setup

    We choose the simplest reliable option for your site, whether that is a Shopify or WordPress integration or a server-side container.

  3. 3

    Build and dedupe

    We send events from your server, match them with browser events and check that each one is counted once.

  4. 4

    Verify and hand over

    We test with real orders or leads, check match quality and show your team how to monitor it.

Questions people ask

How long does it take?

Usually one to three weeks, depending on your website platform and whether offline CRM events are included.

What do I need to provide?

Admin access to your Meta Business Manager, website and Google accounts, and a way for us to place test orders or enquiries.

Does this respect user privacy and consent?

Yes. Personal details are hashed before sending, and we connect the setup to your consent banner so it follows each visitor's choice.

Will my cost per lead go down?

Better data usually helps Meta's targeting and reporting, but results depend on your offer, creative and budget. What you get for sure is a more complete and honest count of your conversions.
