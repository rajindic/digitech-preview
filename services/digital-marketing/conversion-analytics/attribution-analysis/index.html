Attribution analysis, BigQuery export, call tracking

Know which ads, channels and calls actually bring you paying customers

Most businesses spend on Google, Meta and portals without knowing which one really brings customers. Each platform takes credit for the same sale, and phone enquiries are never counted at all. We join your ad data, website analytics, call tracking and CRM into one clear picture, so you can see what each channel really contributes and move your budget with confidence.

What you get

  • A review of how your leads and sales are tracked today, with the gaps listed
  • Call tracking numbers for your ads, website and listings, so phone enquiries are counted by source
  • GA4 data exported to BigQuery, set up and documented in your own Google Cloud account
  • Ads, website, call and CRM data joined into one view of each lead's journey
  • A channel contribution report comparing first-touch, last-touch and data-driven views
  • A simple dashboard showing leads, sales and cost per customer by channel
  • A walkthrough session so your team knows how to read and use the numbers

How it works

  1. 1

    Map your journey

    We list every place leads come from, including ads, Google Business Profile, IndiaMART, JustDial and walk-ins, and check what is tracked today.

  2. 2

    Fix the data

    We set up call tracking, clean UTM tags, the BigQuery export and the link to your CRM or Google Sheet, then test with real enquiries.

  3. 3

    Build the model

    We combine the data and compare attribution views side by side, so you see where each channel helps, not just where the last click came from.

  4. 4

    Review and act

    We walk you through the findings and suggest practical budget shifts, then refresh the report every month if you want.

Questions people ask

How long does this take?

Usually three to five weeks. Tracking needs a few weeks of clean data before the channel comparison becomes reliable, so we start collecting early.

What do I need to give you?

Access to your ad accounts, GA4, website or tag manager, and your CRM or the sheet where you record sales. If sales are offline, a simple way to mark which leads became customers.

Who owns the data and the BigQuery project?

You do. Everything is set up in your own Google and ad accounts, and the call tracking numbers are registered for your business.

Will this tell me exactly which ad made each sale?

Not perfectly, because no tool sees every conversation or WhatsApp chat. It gives you a far more honest picture than the ad platforms alone, which is usually enough to make better budget decisions.
