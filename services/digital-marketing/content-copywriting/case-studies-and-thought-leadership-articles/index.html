Questions people ask

What if my customers do not want to be named?

That is common. We can write anonymised case studies that describe the type of business and the problem without naming anyone.

How much of my time does this take?

Usually one interview of 30 to 45 minutes per piece, plus a review of the draft.

Can you use real numbers?

Only numbers you or your customer confirm and agree to share. We never invent results.

Who owns the content?

You do, including the interview recordings and notes.
