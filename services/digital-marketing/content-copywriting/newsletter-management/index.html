Questions people ask

How often should I send it?

Monthly or twice a month suits most small businesses. We will suggest what fits your audience.

I have only a small list. Is it worth it?

Yes. Even a few hundred customers who know you can bring repeat work and referrals. We can also help you grow the list.

Who owns the list and account?

You do. The email account and every subscriber stay in your name.

What do you need from me each month?

A few minutes to share news or offers and approve the draft. We handle the rest.
