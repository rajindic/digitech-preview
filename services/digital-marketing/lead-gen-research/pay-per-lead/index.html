Questions people ask

Are these leads exclusive to me?

We agree on this upfront. Exclusive leads are sent only to you, while shared lead options are clearly labelled.

What if a lead is fake or wrong?

We agree on a clear invalid-lead policy, such as wrong numbers or out-of-area leads, and replace those as per the agreement.

Do I own the ads and landing pages?

No. In this model the ad accounts and pages are ours. If you want your own ad accounts and assets, our ad management services are a better fit.

How many sales will I get?

That depends on how fast and well your team follows up. We deliver leads that match the agreed criteria, and fast follow-up makes a big difference.
