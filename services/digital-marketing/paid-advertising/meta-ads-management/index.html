Questions people ask

How soon will I get leads?

Lead campaigns often bring enquiries within the first week. Costs usually settle after a few weeks of testing and improvement.

Who owns the ad account and Page?

You do. Everything stays in your Business Manager, billed directly by Meta, and we work through partner access.

Will the leads be good quality?

Lead quality depends on the offer, targeting and form questions. We tune all three and work with your feedback, and pair it with fast WhatsApp follow-up so fewer leads go cold.

What do I need to provide?

Access to your Page and Business Manager, photos or videos of your work or products, your offers and quick feedback on leads.
