Questions people ask

How soon can we launch?

Usually three to four weeks, allowing time to plan, record the first few episodes and set up the show on each platform.

What equipment do I need?

A decent USB microphone and a quiet room are enough to start. We suggest simple options and help you set them up.

Who owns the podcast and recordings?

You do. The show, the accounts and every recording are in your name.

Can you get well-known guests on the show?

We cannot promise who will say yes, but we research well-matched guests and write personal invitations that give you the best chance.
