What you get

  • A shoot plan with shot list, scripts and locations

  • Edited videos for ads, Reels, YouTube and your website

How it works

  1. 1

    Plan the shoot

    We agree what videos you need and why, then write the shot list and short scripts.

    Prepare

    We book the crew and any creators, confirm the location and share a simple checklist for the day.

Questions people ask

How soon can you shoot?

Usually within one to three weeks of agreeing the plan, depending on location, crew and creator availability.

What do I need to arrange?

Access to the location on the shoot day, products or people to film, and someone from your team to help on the day. We handle the rest.

Who owns the footage?

You own the final videos and raw footage. For UGC creators, we agree usage rights upfront so you know where and for how long you can use their videos.

Do you shoot outside major cities?

Yes, in many places. Tell us your location and we will confirm what is possible.
