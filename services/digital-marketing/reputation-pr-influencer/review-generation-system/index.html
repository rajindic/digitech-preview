Review-generation system

Get more 5-star reviews automatically, and hear about problems before they go public

Happy customers rarely leave reviews unless someone asks, and unhappy ones often do. After every visit or order, we send a polite WhatsApp message asking how it went. Customers who loved it get a one-tap link to your review page. Anyone who wasn't happy is sent to you privately, so you can make it right first.

What you get

  • An automatic WhatsApp thank-you and rating request after every visit or order
  • One-tap ratings, with no forms or logins
  • A direct review link for happy customers

How it works

  1. 1

    Connect your customer list

  2. 2

    Write the messages

    We write the thank-you, rating and reminder messages in your tone, and you approve them.

  3. 3

  4. 4

    Go live and report

Questions people ask

Is it allowed to ask customers for reviews?

Yes. Asking every customer politely is fine. We never offer rewards for reviews, never write fake reviews and never stop unhappy customers from reviewing you. We simply give you the chance to fix problems first.

How long does setup take?

Usually about a week, plus any time needed for WhatsApp Business API approval if you don't have it yet.

What do I need to provide?

A way for us to know when a visit or order is complete (your software, a sheet or a simple form your staff fill), your WhatsApp number and your review page link.

Who owns the reviews and customer data?

You do. Reviews are posted on your own business profile, and the customer list and ratings stay in your accounts.

What happens after launch?

We monitor the messages, fix anything that breaks and adjust timing or wording if reply rates drop. You can pause it at any time.
