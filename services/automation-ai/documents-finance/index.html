See one working: Invoice and document extraction (scan to spreadsheet or ERP)

Sample demoMade-up business and data, to show how it works.
  1. 1

    A bill arrives

    A supplier invoice comes in as a phone photo, a scan or a PDF, by WhatsApp, email or a shared folder.

  2. 2

    AI reads the key fields

    Supplier, GSTIN, invoice number, date, taxable value and tax are picked out and copied into one clean row.

  3. 3

    Checks catch mistakes

    The system checks that the totals add up and that this bill has not been entered before.

  4. 4

    Synced to your accounts

    The row goes straight into Tally, Zoho Books or your spreadsheet. No retyping.

  5. 5

    Only unclear bills need a person

    Anything the system is unsure about is flagged, so your team reviews a few bills instead of typing all of them.
