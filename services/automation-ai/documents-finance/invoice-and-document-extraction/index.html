Invoice and document extraction (scan to spreadsheet or ERP)

Stop typing invoices by hand: documents become clean data automatically

Your team spends hours typing purchase bills, delivery challans and receipts into spreadsheets or Tally, and mistakes creep in. We set up an AI system that reads scanned or photographed documents, pulls out the key details such as GSTIN, invoice number, items and tax, and sends them to your spreadsheet or accounting software. Data entry gets much faster, and your team only checks the few documents the system is unsure about.

Questions people ask

How accurate is it?

Accuracy depends on scan quality and document formats. Clear documents are read very reliably, and anything the system is unsure about is flagged for a person to check.

Can it handle handwritten or poor-quality scans?

It can read many of them, but very poor or handwritten documents may need more review. We test with your real samples first.

Is my financial data secure?

We use secure, access-controlled storage, keep data in accounts you control and limit who can see it.

How long does setup take?

Usually two to four weeks, depending on how many document types you have.
