AI receptionist (inbound calls, booking, routing)

Never miss a call: every caller answered, booked or routed, day and night

Missed calls are missed customers, especially when you are busy with patients, clients or on a job. We set up an AI receptionist that answers your inbound calls, handles common questions, books appointments into your calendar and transfers urgent calls to the right person. Callers always get a helpful answer, and your team stops being interrupted for routine questions.

What you get

  • An AI voice receptionist on your existing or new business number
  • Natural conversation in English, Hindi or Hinglish
  • Answers to common questions about timings, location, services and process
  • Appointment booking, rescheduling and cancellations in your calendar
  • Call transfer to the right person for urgent or complex calls
  • WhatsApp or SMS confirmation sent to the caller after booking
  • Call recordings, summaries and a daily report

Questions people ask

Can it work with my current number?

Usually yes. We can forward calls from your existing number, for all calls or only when you do not pick up.

How long does setup take?

Usually two to three weeks.

What about medical or sensitive questions?

The receptionist only shares information you approve. For anything clinical or sensitive, it takes a message or transfers the call to your team.

Who owns the call records?

You do. Recordings, transcripts and bookings stay in your accounts.
