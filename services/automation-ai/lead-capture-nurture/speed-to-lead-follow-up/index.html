Questions people ask

How long does it take to set up?

Usually one to two weeks. The longest part is often Meta approving your WhatsApp Business API number and message templates, which we help you with.

What do I need to provide?

Access to your ad accounts or lead sources, a phone number for WhatsApp, your available time slots and the answers to the questions customers usually ask. We handle the rest.

Who owns the WhatsApp number and the data?

You do. The WhatsApp Business account, the number and every lead stay in your name and your accounts. If you ever stop working with us, everything stays with you.

Will customers know it's automated?

The messages are friendly and clearly come from your business. Whenever a customer asks something unusual, the chat is handed over to you or your team.

What happens after launch?

We keep an eye on it, fix anything that breaks, update the messages when your offers change and send you a monthly report. You can also ask for changes at any time.
