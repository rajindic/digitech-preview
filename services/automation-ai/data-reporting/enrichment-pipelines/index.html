Enrichment pipelines (Clay, Apollo)

Turn a list of company names into researched, ready-to-contact leads

A list of company names or LinkedIn profiles is not much use without the right contact person, a working email and some context. We build enrichment pipelines with tools like Clay and Apollo that find decision-makers, verify their emails and phone numbers, and add useful details like company size and industry. Your sales team spends its time talking to the right people instead of researching them.

What you get

  • A clear definition of your ideal customer and target job roles
  • An automated pipeline in Clay, Apollo or similar tools
  • Decision-maker names, job titles and LinkedIn profiles
  • Verified work emails, and phone numbers where available
  • Company details like size, industry, location and website
  • Optional short research notes or personalised opening lines
  • Clean output to Google Sheets or your CRM, without duplicates

How it works

  1. 1

    Define the target

    We agree which industries, company sizes, locations and roles you want to reach.

  2. 2

    Build the pipeline

    We set up the data sources, lookups and rules in Clay, Apollo or similar tools.

  3. 3

    Verify and clean

    We verify emails, remove duplicates and check a sample by hand for quality.

  4. 4

    Deliver and refresh

    We deliver the list to your sheet or CRM and can re-run the pipeline on new lists.

Questions people ask

Where does the data come from?

From established data providers and public business sources. We verify emails before delivery to reduce bounces.

Is this compliant with privacy laws?

We focus on business contact data for B2B outreach and follow each data provider's rules. We help you include opt-outs in your outreach and avoid personal consumer data.

How long does it take?

The pipeline is usually ready in one to two weeks. After that, new lists can be enriched within days.

Who owns the data and tool accounts?

You do. We can set up Clay or Apollo in your company account, and all enriched data is delivered to you.
