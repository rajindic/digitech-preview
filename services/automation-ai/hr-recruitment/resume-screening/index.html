Hire faster by automating screening, scheduling and onboarding

We automate the routine parts of hiring with AI that screens resumes against your criteria, books interviews, runs short pre-screen calls and handles onboarding steps. Questions people ask

Will AI reject good candidates?

AI follows the criteria you set, and we keep humans in the loop for final decisions. We review results together early on and adjust to avoid unfair filtering.

Is candidate data kept private?

Yes. Data stays in your systems or secure accounts in your name, with access limited to your team, and we follow sensible privacy practices.

How long does setup take?

Usually three to six weeks, depending on how many steps you want to automate and which tools you use.

Does it work with Naukri, LinkedIn or our ATS?

Often yes. We connect where these tools allow it, or use email and exports where they do not.
