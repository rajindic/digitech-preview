WhatsApp commerce chain

Recover carts, confirm COD orders and take payments, all on WhatsApp

Online stores lose sales to abandoned carts, fake COD orders and customers asking where their order is. We connect your store to WhatsApp so customers get cart reminders, COD confirmation messages, order and shipping updates, and UPI payment links right in the chat. You win back more carts, ship fewer COD parcels that come back, and answer fewer order-status questions.

What you get

  • Abandoned cart reminders on WhatsApp with a link back to checkout
  • COD order confirmation with one-tap confirm or cancel, before you ship
  • An option to switch COD orders to prepaid with UPI or a payment link
  • Order, shipping and delivery updates from Shopify, WooCommerce and Shiprocket
  • In-chat catalogue and payment for repeat orders
  • Message templates approved by Meta
  • A dashboard of recovered carts, confirmed orders and replies

How it works

  1. 1

    Connect the store

    We link your store, shipping partner and payment gateway to your WhatsApp Business number.

  2. 2

    Write the messages

    We write each message in your brand voice, in English, Hindi or Hinglish, and get templates approved.

  3. 3

    Test the chain

    We run test orders through every step, from cart to delivery, before going live.

  4. 4

    Go live and tune

    We watch results in the first weeks and adjust timing and wording to improve them.

Questions people ask

Which stores do you support?

Shopify, WooCommerce and most custom stores with an API, along with Shiprocket and other shipping partners.

Will this reduce RTO?

Confirming COD orders before shipping and nudging customers to pay online usually helps reduce returns. Results depend on your products and customers, and we track them with you.

How long does setup take?

Usually one to two weeks, including WhatsApp template approval.

Who owns the customer data?

You do. Everything runs on your own WhatsApp Business account and your store.
