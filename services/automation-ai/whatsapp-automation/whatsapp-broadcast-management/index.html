Questions people ask

Can you send to any number?

Only to people who have agreed to hear from you. This protects your number and is what WhatsApp requires.

Which platform do you use?

We work with the platform you already use, like Interakt, AiSensy or WATI, or help you pick one.

How often should we send?

Usually two to six campaigns a month, depending on your business. Sending too often leads to blocks, so we plan carefully.

Who owns the contacts?

You do. Your contact list and WhatsApp account remain in your name at all times.
