Single workflow (form to CRM to WhatsApp/Slack)

Take one repetitive task off your team's plate for good

Most businesses have one task that eats up time every day, like copying form enquiries into a sheet and then messaging the team about them. We automate that one process end to end, for example from your website form or Facebook lead ad into your CRM, with an instant WhatsApp or Slack alert to the right person. It is a quick, low-risk way to see what automation can do for you.
