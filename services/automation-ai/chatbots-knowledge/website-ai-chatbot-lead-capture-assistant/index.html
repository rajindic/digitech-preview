Website AI chatbot / lead-capture assistant

Answer every website visitor instantly, even at midnight, and turn them into leads

Visitors leave when they can't get a quick answer. We add an AI assistant to your website that knows your services, timings and FAQs. Questions people ask

Will it give wrong answers or make things up?

It answers only from the information you approve. When it doesn't know, it says so and offers a callback from your team. Prices and anything sensitive are always passed to a person.

How long does it take?

Usually one to two weeks, depending on how much information you have ready and how many tools it needs to connect to.

What do I need to provide?

Your website access (or your web developer's), your service details and FAQs, and where you want leads to go: WhatsApp, email, a sheet or your CRM.

Does it work on any website?

Yes. It works on WordPress, Shopify, Wix and custom-built sites. We add one small snippet of code, and it won't slow your site down.

Who owns the chats and leads?

You do. Conversations and leads are stored in your own accounts, and you can export them at any time.
