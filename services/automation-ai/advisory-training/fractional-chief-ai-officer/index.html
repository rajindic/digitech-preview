Senior AI guidance for your business, without a full-time hire

Questions people ask

How much time do you spend with us?

We agree a set number of sessions and availability each month, based on how much is happening in your business.

Do you build things too?

The role is advisory, but our team can build projects if you choose, and we stay objective when reviewing other vendors.

What do I need to provide?

Access to your leadership team, openness about your goals, and one person who owns AI work internally.

Can we stop or change the engagement?

Yes. It is a flexible retainer, and we agree notice terms upfront.
