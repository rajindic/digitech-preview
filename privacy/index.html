Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Digitech collects through this website, our forms, WhatsApp and our live demo, why we collect it, who sees it, how long we keep it and what control you have. We have written it in plain language and it follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

In short

  • We collect only what you give us (name, business, phone number, email, what you need) plus basic technical data needed to run and protect the site.
  • We use it to reply to you, send a quote or audit, run the live demo you ask for, and keep our records. We do not sell your data and we do not show you ads based on it.
  • We share it only with the service providers that help us run these things, such as email, spreadsheet, messaging and hosting providers.
  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data, or stop messages, at any time. On WhatsApp, reply STOP.
  • You can reach our grievance officer at any time. Details are at the end of this page.

Why we use your data

We use your personal data only for these purposes:

  • to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote, audit or proposal, and follow up on it;
  • to provide, bill and support the services you buy from us, and to keep business, tax and accounting records;
  • to run the live demo you ask for and send the messages that are part of it;
  • to keep the website and our systems secure, detect spam and fraud, and fix faults;
  • to meet legal duties, and to establish or defend our rights in a dispute.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We do not use it to build advertising profiles. We do not make decisions about you using fully automated profiling. We will not use your data for a new purpose without asking you again first.

Our legal basis under the DPDP Act is your consent, which you give by ticking or submitting a form, messaging us first or starting the demo. For some uses the Act allows us to proceed without fresh consent, for example where you voluntarily gave us your details for a purpose and have not objected, to meet a legal obligation, or to respond to a medical or safety emergency. We rely on that only where it genuinely applies.

Your consent and how to withdraw it

You are always free to say no, and to change your mind. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to hello@example.com, messaging us on WhatsApp, or replying STOP to any WhatsApp message from us.

Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it. After you withdraw, we stop using your data for the purpose you withdrew from and delete it unless the law requires us to keep it. Withdrawal does not affect what we did with your data lawfully before then. It may mean we can no longer provide a service that needs that data, and we will tell you if so.

WhatsApp and other messages

  • We message you on WhatsApp only if you gave us your number and asked for a reply, a quote, an audit or the live demo, or if you messaged us first.
  • Messages are for that purpose only. We do not send you unrelated promotions without your separate permission.
  • Reply STOP at any time and we will stop messaging you, including automated follow-ups and reminders.
  • Reply call me, or write to us, to ask for a person or to ask us to delete your details.
  • WhatsApp is operated by Meta. Meta processes messages under its own terms and privacy policy, which we do not control. Messages in WhatsApp are end-to-end encrypted in transit, but our business account tools can read the messages sent to our number.
  • The demo is run on a pretend business. Your answers in it are used only for the demo and for our own follow-up if you ask for it.

Cookies and similar technology

At the time of writing this website does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies or tracking pixels, and does not store information in your browser to track you. Our hosting and bot-check providers may set strictly necessary cookies or use similar technical signals to keep the site secure and stop spam. You can block cookies in your browser settings, but the forms may not work if the bot-check cannot run.

If we later add website analytics (for example Google Analytics), we will first update this page and show a clear notice with a choice, so you can accept or refuse non-essential cookies.

Storing data outside India

Some of our providers (for example Google, Cloudflare and Meta) run servers in several countries, so your data may be processed or stored outside India. The DPDP Act allows transfer to any country except those the Government of India restricts by notification. We do not transfer your data to any such restricted country, and we choose providers with recognised security standards.

How we protect data

We take reasonable security safeguards, as the DPDP Act and the IT Act require. These include:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS) for this website and its forms;
  • bot-checks and a hidden spam trap on every form;
  • limiting access to leads to the people who need them, protected by strong sign-ins;
  • keeping secrets such as keys and tokens out of the website code;
  • cleaning form data before it is saved, so it cannot run as a formula or script;
  • scheduled deletion of old demo data.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach happens that affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires, explain what happened and what you can do, and fix the cause.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Know and access: get a summary of the personal data we hold about you, what we do with it, and who we have shared it with;
  • Correct, complete and update data that is wrong, incomplete or out of date;
  • Erase your data when it is no longer needed for the purpose, or when you withdraw consent, unless the law requires us to keep it;
  • Withdraw consent at any time (see above);
  • Get grievances redressed quickly by us, before going anywhere else;
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to.

To use any of these rights, write to us at hello@example.com or message us on WhatsApp, saying who you are (the name and number you used) and what you want. We may ask you to confirm it is really you before we act, so that nobody else can see or delete your data. There is no charge. We aim to reply within 7 days and to complete your request within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational. Please use our grievance process first, as the DPDP Act expects.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data of children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us their details, write to us and we will delete them. We do not track or monitor children, or show them targeted advertising.

Data we handle for our clients

When we build or run something for a business, such as a website form, a WhatsApp assistant or a CRM, that business decides why your data is collected and is the Data Fiduciary. We act as its Data Processor: we handle your data only on its instructions, under a written agreement, and with the same security safeguards. For anything about how that business uses your data, please contact the business directly. If you contact us instead, we will pass your request to them or help you reach them.
