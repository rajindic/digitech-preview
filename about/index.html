Why we exist

Most small and mid-sized businesses in India do good work but lose customers in the gaps: a lead that arrives at 11 pm and is answered the next afternoon, a website nobody can find on Google, leads scattered across WhatsApp, Excel and notebooks, ads that cost money without anyone knowing which ones work.

We started Digitech to close those gaps with simple, dependable tools that you can see working before you commit. We do not sell packages of jargon. We find the few things that will make the biggest difference to your business, build them properly, and keep an eye on the numbers.

What we believe

The promises behind every project.

You own everything

Your domain, website, ad accounts, WhatsApp number, data and content stay in your name, even if you stop working with us.

Plain words, clear plans

No jargon and no padding. You get a short plan that says what we will do, why, and what you need to give us.

Quotes, not price lists

Every business needs a different mix, so we quote for exactly what you need. You never pay for things you will not use.

Honest about results

We do not promise rankings or a fixed number of leads, because nobody honestly can. We promise good work, clear reports and fixing our own mistakes.

See it before you buy

Every service is shown as a working demo with made-up data, so you can see how it behaves before you talk to us.

People stay in charge

We use AI tools to build and run things faster, and a person checks the work before it goes live. Automations always hand over to a human when needed.
